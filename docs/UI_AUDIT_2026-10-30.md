## Anti-patterns verdict

**Fail on the product surface, pass on the landing page.** Nobody would say AI
made `/`, `/pricing` or `/chat`. The tells are concentrated:

- `components/layout/Auth.tsx:27` — `bg-clip-text bg-gradient-to-r` headline,
  plus a `TRAVEL OS` tracked-uppercase pill and a "Native iOS + Android coming
  soon" tile that the September audit already asked to remove. This wraps
  sign-in, sign-up, forgot and reset password.
- `components/itinerary/StopCard.tsx:9-19`, `components/results/ItineraryResults.tsx:108-118`,
  `routes/profiles/index.tsx:264-280`, `components/chat/ChatSidebar.tsx:109,229,306` —
  emoji as category badges, budget markers and sidebar icons. `DESIGN.md`
  bans emoji in nav, quick prompts and popups; 113 emoji in 26 files.
- `routes/about.tsx:192-206` — three `border-l-4` side-stripe items, a pastel
  three-card row with nested cards, hex colours, and body copy pasted from the
  README ("Personalized City Discovery 🗺️✨ powered by Catppuccin Latte hues").
- Tracked-uppercase eyebrows in 31 files (47 hits). `DESIGN.md` names one
  Space Mono `.kicker` as a brand system, which is voice; the same kicker above
  every card in Settings (`Tags.tsx`, `Interests.tsx`, `CityInfoHeader.tsx`)
  is scaffolding.
- `rounded-3xl` on 35 elements in 21 files, most in `routes/settings/index.tsx`,
  `routes/settings/memory.tsx` and `routes/journal/trip/[tripId].tsx`. Cards
  top out at `--radius` (0.8 rem).
- 14 hand-rolled `fixed inset-0` overlays beside 7 uses of `ui/dialog` /
  `ui/sheet`. Two modal vocabularies on one surface.

## Executive summary

- Audit Health Score: **14/20** (Good)
- Issues: P0 0 · P1 9 · P2 11 · P3 4
- Top issues:
  1. Sign-in and sign-up inputs are not associated with their labels; the
     password reveal button has no accessible name; two `h1` per page.
  2. The nav's primary links are rendered only after auth resolves
     (`Nav.tsx:119` `<Show when={!isLoading()}>`), so the server HTML has no
     navigation and the links pop in on every public page.
  3. The `Loci` wordmark uses Tailwind `font-serif`, which resolves to
     `ui-serif` (Times/Georgia), on every page.
  4. Four confirmed contrast failures: `/pricing` "coming later" list, the
     `/discover` hero hint, the active nav item, and the PWA install button in
     dark mode.
  5. Settings forms (`TravelProfiles.tsx`, `Tags.tsx`, `Interests.tsx`,
     `TasteAndPrivacy.tsx`) have 29 inputs with no label, id or aria-label.
- Next steps, in order: `$impeccable harden` (labels, names, nav SSR),
  `$impeccable colorize` (contrast + off-token palette), `$impeccable quieter`
  (auth and about tells), `$impeccable distill` (orphans, duplicate modals),
  `$impeccable polish`.

## Findings by severity

### P1

**[P1] Form controls without bound labels on auth pages**

- Location: `components/features/Auth/SignIn.tsx` (0 `<label>`, 1 `for=`), `SignUp.tsx`, `ForgotPassword.tsx`, `ResetPassword.tsx`; wrapper `components/layout/Auth.tsx`
- Category: Accessibility
- Impact: Screen readers announce "edit text" with no name for email and password; the "Remember me" checkbox and the password reveal `<button>` (32×40, no text, no `aria-label`) are anonymous. Browser probe on `/auth/signin`: 3 unlabeled inputs, 1 unnamed button, 2 `h1`.
- WCAG: 1.3.1, 3.3.2, 4.1.2
- Recommendation: Use the Kobalte `TextField` (`ui/textfield.tsx`) `Label` so `for`/`id` are generated; give the reveal button `aria-label="Show password"` with `aria-pressed`; demote the marketing headline in `Auth.tsx` to `h2`.
- Command: `$impeccable harden auth`

**[P1] Settings inputs without labels**

- Location: `components/features/Settings/TravelProfiles.tsx:590,624,751` and 17 more; `Tags.tsx:238,258,423,430`; `Interests.tsx:221,385`; `TasteAndPrivacy.tsx:62,80`; `components/journal/JournalDay.tsx:67`
- Category: Accessibility
- Impact: 29 of 74 `<input>` sites have no `id`, `aria-label` or wrapping `<label>` within reach. The two 1200-line settings files the September audit flagged are where they sit.
- WCAG: 1.3.1, 4.1.2
- Recommendation: Wrap each control in the `TextField` / `Checkbox` primitives from `ui/`; add `aria-label` to the search/filter inputs that have only a placeholder.
- Command: `$impeccable harden settings`

**[P1] Primary navigation is gated on auth loading**

- Location: `components/Nav.tsx:119` (`<Show when={!isLoading()}>`), `:231`, `:268`
- Category: Accessibility / Performance
- Impact: Server-rendered HTML carries the logo and nothing else; links appear after the `/me` round trip. Observed on `/`, `/discover`, `/auth/signin` and `/chat` at first paint (nav with only the logo in every screenshot before hydration). Crawlers see no site navigation; users get a layout shift; if the API is slow or down, public pages have no nav at all.
- WCAG: 2.4.5 (indirectly), CLS
- Recommendation: Render `publicItems` immediately and swap to `journeyItems` when `isAuthenticated()` resolves. Reserve the account slot width so the switch does not shift.
- Command: `$impeccable harden nav`

**[P1] Wordmark renders in the system serif**

- Location: `components/Nav.tsx` (`font-serif text-xl ... Loci`), one `<p class="font-serif text-2xl">Loci</p>` (chat sidebar), `components/Footer.tsx`
- Category: Theming
- Impact: Tailwind's `font-serif` resolves to `ui-serif` because `tailwind.config.cjs` has no `fontFamily` extension. The brand mark in the nav is in Times on every page. The probe reports `ui-serif` on 2–3 nodes per route.
- Recommendation: Replace `font-serif` with the existing `.font-display` utility (`base.css`), or add `fontFamily.serif = ["Fraunces", "Georgia", "serif"]` to the Tailwind config so the 19 `font-serif` / `font-mono` sites resolve to brand faces.
- Command: `$impeccable typeset`

**[P1] Contrast failures (measured, light mode unless noted)**

- Location and ratio:
  - `routes/pricing.tsx` "Offline trips…", "Speech-to-text…", "24/7 personal agent", "Not available yet…" — 3.82:1 at 14 px and 12 px (`text-muted-foreground/70`-class grey on card)
  - `routes/discover.tsx` "e.g. Évora vs Beja from Porto" — 2.49:1 at 12 px (`text-muted-foreground` on the dark hero: grey on colour)
  - `components/Nav.tsx` active item ("Pricing", "About") — 3.95:1 at 14 px (`text-primary` on `bg-primary/10`)
  - `components/PWAInstall.tsx` "Install App" in dark mode — 3.43:1 at 12 px (hard-coded blue)
- Category: Accessibility / Theming
- WCAG: 1.4.3
- Recommendation: Drop the opacity modifier on muted text (`text-muted-foreground` is already tuned to 5.6:1); on dark panels use `hsl(var(--hero-foreground) / 0.8)` instead of the page's muted grey; active nav text at `--foreground` with the tint as the only indicator; PWA button on `--primary`.
- Command: `$impeccable colorize`

**[P1] Gradient text, eyebrow pill and "coming soon" tile on every auth page**

- Location: `components/layout/Auth.tsx:21-33`, tile at `:~60` ("Native iOS + Android coming soon")
- Category: Anti-pattern
- Impact: The one page every new user passes through carries three tells `DESIGN.md` bans, and a promise with no date that the September audit already listed for removal.
- Recommendation: Solid `--foreground` headline with the second line in `--primary`; remove the pill; remove the tile until there is a TestFlight link (the landing page already shows "BETA · TESTFLIGHT", so the copy also contradicts itself).
- Command: `$impeccable quieter auth`

**[P1] Off-token Tailwind palette in live components**

- Location (hits): `components/ui/ActionToolbar.tsx` (30; imported by `ResultsPage` and four preview routes) — `bg-blue-50`, `text-gray-600`; `components/shared/FavoriteButton.tsx` (`bg-red-50`, `text-gray-300`; 6 importers); `components/ui/SelectionToolbar.tsx` (4 importers); `components/PWAInstall.tsx` (blue); `components/UpgradePrompt.tsx` (`z-[100]`, `bg-black/40`); `components/ui/CityInfoHeader.tsx` (`text-slate-200`-class labels in the hero, 4 eyebrows); `routes/billing/index.tsx` (hex + rgba)
- Category: Theming
- Impact: These are the pieces that sit on top of results and itineraries, so the "generic shadcn/zinc" drift the last audit named is most visible exactly where users act. Detector: 11 gray-on-colour hits in 3 files.
- Recommendation: Map to tokens: `bg-blue-50` → `bg-primary/5`, `text-gray-*` → `text-muted-foreground` / `text-foreground`, `bg-red-50` → `bg-destructive/10`, `z-[100]` → the `z-50` modal slot.
- Command: `$impeccable colorize results`

**[P1] Emoji as category badges and sidebar icons**

- Location: `components/itinerary/StopCard.tsx:9-19`, `components/results/ItineraryResults.tsx:108-126`, `routes/profiles/index.tsx:264-280`, `components/chat/ChatSidebar.tsx:109,229,306`, `components/features/Recents/CitiesView.tsx:144-173`, `routes/trip-setup.tsx` (11)
- Category: Anti-pattern
- Impact: Visible on `/preview/itinerary` (📍 🛍️ 🍽️ beside every stop, 🗺️ in the section heading). Renders differently per OS, cannot be themed, and `DESIGN.md` names it as an anti-pattern.
- Recommendation: One lucide icon per category through a single `categoryIcon()` helper shared by `StopCard` and `ItineraryResults` (they currently duplicate the mapping).
- Command: `$impeccable quieter itinerary`

**[P1] Map chunk loaded by non-map surfaces**

- Location: build output `useMapLifecycle-*.js` 1 784 KB raw / 485 KB gzip; imported by `DetailedItemModal`, `PlaceDetail`, `ResultsPage`, `activities`, `restaurants`, `Globe`, `TripGlobe`, `_slug_` and two `index` chunks
- Category: Performance
- Impact: Opening a place detail or the results page pulls mapbox-gl even when the list view is showing. The route itself is code-split, so this is per-surface, not on `/`.
- Recommendation: `lazy()` the `Map` component behind the list/map toggle (`SplitView`) so the chunk is requested only when the map pane mounts; keep `atmosphere.ts` / layer modules out of shared helpers imported by list-only components.
- Command: `$impeccable optimize results`

## Patterns and systemic issues

- **Orphans accumulate.** Ten unimported components hold most of the off-token CSS. The September audit deleted eight; the same shape is back.
- **Opacity instead of tokens** for secondary text, which is why contrast passes and fails on identical classes.
- **Tailwind config has no `fontFamily`**, so every `font-serif` / `font-mono` falls to system faces. This caused the wordmark bug and would cause more.
- **Settings is where labels go missing**: the two largest files have the most unlabeled inputs. Splitting them (deferred in September) is now also an accessibility task.
- **Chat and results are on-system; auth, about and settings are not.** The restyle "enforce `DESIGN.md` per page group" is roughly half done.

## Positive findings

- `themes.css` documents every ratio it claims, and the dark palette measured clean on `/discover`, `/pricing`, `/preview/itinerary`, `/chat` and `/about` (one failure: the PWA button).
- Global `min-h-[44px]` on buttons and inputs holds: every button measured at 390 px was ≥ 44 px tall.
- `:focus-visible` is global with `--ring` at 3.9:1; reduced-motion overrides cover the stagger, marquee, press and Kobalte accordion.
- No horizontal overflow on any of six routes at 390 px; the mobile nav sheet has 44 px rows.
- Landing, pricing and chat are distinctive and on-brand; the chat composer, quick prompts and the Loci mascot read as one product.
- Lighthouse CI is wired with a ratchet (`lighthouserc.cjs`), fonts are preloaded with `display=swap`, posthog and three are dynamic imports, 248 `aria-label`s across 130 files.
